About me

Curious about circulation.

Based atIISER Pune, India
FieldEarth & Climate Science
FocusRegional Hadley circulation

I am a fifth-year PhD student in the Department of Earth and Climate Science at IISER Pune, working under the supervision of Dr. Neena Joseph Mani.

My research examines the variability of regional Hadley circulations and the processes that modulate them—including scale interactions and tropical–extratropical influences. I am originally from Assam, India, and completed an Integrated M.Sc. in Physics at Dibrugarh University in 2019.
