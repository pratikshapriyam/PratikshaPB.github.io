Hi there, I’m Pratiksha 👋

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Utah with a background in Earth and climate science. My research focuses on regional Hadley circulations, tropical variability, and tropical–extratropical interactions.

I’m now applying this scientific foundation to climate risk management, financial risk, and reinsurance—building reproducible Python workflows that connect physical hazards with portfolio decisions.

What I work with

  • Climate and reanalysis datasets: NetCDF, ERA5, gridded observations
  • Scientific Python: Xarray, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib
  • Risk analytics: hazard, exposure, vulnerability, loss and scenario analysis
  • Geospatial workflows and clear scientific communication
